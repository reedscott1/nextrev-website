2. Services Description

Next Rev develops software for point-of-sale systems. Our current products include:

2.1 Member Link

A POS application for merchants with private memberships. Member Link lets staff scan a membership card to open a tab and attach a member to an in-progress order from the POS payment screen.

  • Runs on the POS device
  • Connects to the Platform's REST API to read customer records, read and write orders, and read merchant configuration (order types)
  • No account or login required beyond your merchant account on the Platform
  • Does not transmit data to Next Rev or any third party

2.2 Terminal Settlement Report

A POS application that produces a per-shift cash drawer reconciliation receipt for bartenders.

  • Runs on the POS device
  • Connects to the Platform's REST API to read employee shift records, cash drawer events, and the bartender's orders, payments, and refunds (read-only)
  • Prints the settlement receipt to the POS device's built-in receipt printer
  • Does not persist any data on the device — the report is computed on demand
  • No account or login required beyond your merchant account on the Platform
  • Does not transmit data to Next Rev or any third party
